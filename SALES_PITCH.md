## 🔴 The Problem

### India's Land Verification Crisis

| Statistic | Value |
|---|---|
| 🏘️ Disputed land cases in Indian courts | **66 million+** |
| 💸 Annual loss due to land fraud | **₹40,000+ crore** |
| 👨‍🌾 Farmers without clear title documents | **~40%** |
| 📋 Time to complete land mutation (UP) | **45–180 days** |
| 🏦 Bank NPA from fraudulent land collateral | **₹8,000+ crore/year** |
| 🤝 Families torn apart by property disputes | **Millions annually** |

### The Pain Points

**👨‍🌾 For Farmers & Rural Citizens:**
- Cannot understand complex land records (in English or archaic Hindi)
- Travel 50–100 km to Tehsil office to check basic records
- Pay ₹500–5,000 bribe to get simple documents
- Cannot detect encumbrances before buying land

**🏦 For Banks & NBFCs:**
- No fast way to verify land collateral genuineness
- Manual field verification costs ₹2,000–10,000 per property
- Takes 15–30 days for due diligence
- Fraudulent land used as collateral for loans

**🏠 For Real Estate Buyers:**
- Cannot check ongoing court disputes before buying
- Boundary disputes discovered only after purchase
- No single platform for complete land intelligence

**⚖️ For Lawyers & Revenue Officers:**
- Fragmented data across 30+ government portals
- No AI to assist in dispute risk assessment
- Manual boundary measurement is slow and inaccurate

### 🏦 2. Bank Lien & Encumbrance Detection
- Integrates with CERSAI, State Revenue portals

- Risk score from 0–100

### ⚖️ 3. AI Dispute Risk Assessment
- Cross-references eCourts database

### 📐 4. GPS Demarcation
- Multi-GNSS (GPS + GLONASS + NavIC) accuracy
- Kalman filter for ±50cm accuracy
